# Contributing to Tunnet

## Contributor agreement required

Tunnet accepts a contribution only after the applicable contributor agreement
has been validly signed through Tunnet's designated signing system.

- Sign the Individual Contributor Assignment and License Agreement when You
  personally own the contribution.
- An authorized representative must sign the Corporate Contributor Assignment
  and License Agreement when an employer or another entity owns or controls
  the contribution.

Do not add a name to `CONTRIBUTORS` as a substitute for signing. A pull-request
statement is not sufficient unless the designated signing system records it as
a valid electronic signature.

## License applied to accepted code

The public license of an accepted file is determined by `LICENSING.md` and its
file-level SPDX metadata. The contributor agreement gives Tunnet the rights
needed to maintain the component-level model, offer commercial alternatives,
and relicense components in the future.

## Third-party and generated material

A contribution must clearly disclose:

- copied or adapted third-party code, documentation, media, or data;
- the source and exact license of that material;
- substantial AI-generated or tool-generated material when provenance or
  ownership may be relevant;
- code written for an employer, client, school, or another party that may own
  rights in it.

Do not submit confidential information, credentials, malicious code, or
material that You are not authorized to contribute.

## Review requirements

All changes require review, tests appropriate to the component, and successful
license-compliance checks. Tunnet may reject any contribution for technical,
legal, security, or product reasons.
